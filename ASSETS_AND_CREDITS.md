## 3. 3D Environmental & Vehicle Assets

- **Moon Base Alpha Habitat Modules:** Modular pressurized habitats, airlocks, observation cupolas, and interconnecting corridors modeled for extreme vacuum environments.
- **Scientific Lunar Rover:** Custom 6-wheel rover chassis with low center of mass, dual high-output headlights, front telemetry sensor mast, roll cage, and 6 independent sprung suspension assemblies.
- **Memorial Replicas:**
  - **Apollo 11 Memorial:** Commemorative 1969 lunar descent stage, American flag, and replica historic plaque (*"We came in peace for all mankind"*).
  - **Tiranga Lunar Memorial:** Commemorative 2023 Chandrayaan-3 lunar lander, Indian Tricolor flag (Tiranga), and historic lunar south pole landing marker.
- **Lunar Terrain:** Procedurally sculpted regolith surface featuring crater basins, impact rim palisades, and 41 registered geological target boulders.
