## 2. Planned Future Milestones

### Milestone 1: Multi-Crew Rover Operations & Utility Trailer (Phase 10)
- **Status:** *Planned*
- **Description:** Upgrade the Scientific Lunar Rover to support cooperative two-player expeditions:
  - **Co-Pilot Science Station:** A passenger seat equipped with an interactive ground-penetrating radar interface to detect subsurface voids and buried regolith layers.
  - **Towed Utility Trailer:** A physics-constrained cargo trailer attached via a universal ball socket to transport larger geological core samples and replacement solar batteries.

### Milestone 2: Real-Time Solar System Orrery & NASA Horizons Integration (Phase 11)
- **Status:** *Planned*
- **Description:** Connect the in-game astronomical observatory to NASA's **JPL Horizons Ephemeris API**:
  - Live 3D planetary positions for Earth, Moon, Sun, Mars, and Jupiter calculated in real time.
  - Accurate lunar phase tracking matching the true celestial calendar.
  - Educational time-lapse controls allowing students to simulate eclipse cycles and planetary alignments.

### Milestone 3: Subsurface Ice Core-Drilling in Shadowed Craters (Phase 12)
- **Status:** *Planned*
- **Description:** Implement a specialized mini-game centered on Permanently Shadowed Regions (PSRs):
  - Astronauts navigate into pitch-black polar craters using rover spotlights.
  - Deploy a rotary percussion drill to extract water ice cores from cold-trap permafrost.
  - Connects to NASA's VIPER (Volatiles Investigating Polar Exploration Rover) mission objectives.

### Milestone 4: Mars Habitat Expansion (Phase 13)
- **Status:** *Planned Concept*
- **Description:** Introduce an interconnected secondary planetary map situated in Jezero Crater on Mars:
  - **Martian Physics:** Adjusts gravity to `74.5 studs/s²` (0.38g).
  - **Atmospheric Pressure:** Thin carbon dioxide atmosphere requiring MOXIE (Mars Oxygen ISRU Experiment) oxygen extraction.
  - **Global Dust Storms:** Dynamic weather events that reduce solar array efficiency and challenge rover battery management.

### Milestone 5: Educator Portal & Classroom Dashboard
- **Status:** *Planned*
- **Description:** A dedicated web and in-game teacher console:
  - Real-time roster dashboard showing student mission completion rates and badge awards.
  - "Teacher Action" triggers to manually activate a simulated solar storm drill during class presentations.
  - Printable PDF certificates matching the in-game handwritten Certificate of Lunar Exploration.
